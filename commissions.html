What this is

I write and build short animated stories that run entirely inside a web page. They are not video — nothing buffers, nothing streams, nothing needs a good connection. They are not an app, so nobody has to install anything or agree to anything.

Each one is a single file. It can live on your site, behind a QR code on a wall, in an email, or on a card in someone's hand. If they can open a link, they can read it.

Where it can live

Each piece is one file, around 40–80KB — smaller than a single photograph. No framework, no plugin, no tracker, nothing to keep updated. That makes it unusually portable:

  • On your own site — uploaded like any other page, or dropped inside an existing one so visitors never leave your domain.
  • In your CMS — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and the rest all take it as an upload or an embed.
  • Behind a QR code — on a wall, a label, a leaflet or a ticket. It opens on whatever phone the person already has.
  • Offline, on a kiosk or a USB stick — no wifi, no signal, no connection of any kind. This is the one that matters in galleries and rural venues, where most digital interpretation stops working the moment the network does.
  • Or hosted by me — if you would rather not host anything, I can put it somewhere and hand you a link.

It works on old phones and slow connections, needs no app and no account, and collects no data about anybody who opens it. You own the file outright — there is no platform to be locked into and nothing that expires.

Scale and budget

A single short piece, a series, or a set of linked stories for one site or campaign. Projects typically run from a few weeks to a few months.

Budgets start in the low thousands for a single commissioned piece and scale with length, cast and accessibility scope. If you have a fixed budget, say so at the start and I'll tell you honestly what fits inside it.

Grant-funded projects welcome — I'm happy to be costed into a bid before it's submitted, and to provide what you need for the application.
